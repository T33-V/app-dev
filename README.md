# My Favorite Series: Peaky Blinders

> "By order of the Peaky Blinders!"

**Peaky Blinders** is an epic crime drama television series created by *Steven Knight*. Set in Birmingham, England, it follows the exploits of the Shelby crime family in the direct aftermath of the First World War.

### The Shelby Family
* **Cillian Murphy** as Thomas "Tommy" Shelby (The Leader)
* **Helen McCrory** as Elizabeth "Polly" Gray (The Matriarch)
* **Paul Anderson** as Arthur Shelby Jr. (The Muscle)
* **Joe Cole** as John Shelby (The Third Brother)

### Key Themes & Milestones
1. **Post-War Trauma**: Exploring how WWI veterans integrated back into society.
2. **Industrial Birmingham**: Navigating the gritty streets, legal horse racing bets, and illegal weapon trade.
3. **Political Rise**: Watching the family move from street-level gangsters to high-ranking political figures.
